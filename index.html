David
Pinto Ramos

Postdoctoral researcher · CASUS–HZDR
Dynamics of Complex Living Systems · Görlitz, Germany

I am a theoretical physicist working on nonlinear dynamics and pattern formation. I study how broken symmetries and interactions shape collective behaviour in spatially extended systems, from coupled oscillators to plant populations.

My work combines bifurcation and stability analysis with simulations of continuum and individual-based models, with dryland ecosystems as a principal application.
